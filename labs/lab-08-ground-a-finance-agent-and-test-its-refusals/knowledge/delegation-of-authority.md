# Northstar Finance — Delegation of Authority

## 1. Purpose
This document states who may commit Northstar Finance to expenditure, approve
payments, and make decisions affecting customers.

## 2. Expenditure authority
| Value (SGD) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 5,000 | Department head |
| 5,000.01 - 50,000 | Financial Controller |
| 50,000.01 - 250,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| Above 250,000 | Board Finance Committee |

## 3. Payment release
Payments require **two authorised signatories**. The preparer of a payment may
never be one of its approvers.

## 4. Credit and collections authority
| Decision | Approver |
|---|---|
| Credit limit up to SGD 100,000 | Credit Manager |
| Credit limit above SGD 100,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| Write-off up to SGD 25,000 | Financial Controller |
| Write-off above SGD 25,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| Legal escalation of a debt | Chief Financial Officer and Legal Counsel |

## 5. Authority that may never be delegated to an AI agent
The following decisions require a named human with delegated authority. An AI
agent may prepare, summarise or recommend, but must not decide:

- Approving or posting any journal entry.
- Releasing any payment.
- Granting, changing or withdrawing a customer credit limit.
- Writing off a receivable.
- Sending any external communication to a customer, regulator or auditor.
- Interpreting a regulatory obligation or making a disclosure decision.

## 6. Escalation
Where authority is unclear, escalate to the Financial Controller. Absence of an
explicit prohibition is **not** authority to act.
