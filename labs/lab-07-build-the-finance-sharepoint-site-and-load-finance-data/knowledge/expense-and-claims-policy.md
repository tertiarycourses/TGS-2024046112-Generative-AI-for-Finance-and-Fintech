# Northstar Finance — Expense and Claims Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FIN-001
**Effective date:** 1 January 2026
**Review date:** 31 December 2026
**Owner:** Financial Controller
**Classification:** Internal

> Synthetic policy created for training (TGS-2026065050). Not a real policy.

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out how Northstar Finance staff incur, claim and approve
business expenses, and the evidence required for each claim.

## 2. Scope
All employees, contractors and interns of Northstar Finance in Singapore,
Malaysia, Hong Kong and Australia.

## 3. Submission deadlines
- Claims must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense date.
- Claims older than 60 days require Financial Controller approval.
- Claims older than 90 days are not payable.

## 4. Approval thresholds
| Claim value (SGD) | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 250 | Line manager |
| 250.01 - 2,000 | Department head |
| 2,000.01 - 10,000 | Financial Controller |
| Above 10,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

Approval must be given by someone senior to the claimant. Self-approval is
prohibited at every value.

## 5. Evidence requirements
Every claim requires an itemised receipt showing vendor, date, amount and
currency. A credit-card statement line is **not** sufficient evidence.
Claims without a compliant receipt are rejected.

## 6. Category rules
- **Travel** — economy class for flights under 5 hours; premium economy above.
- **Accommodation** — up to SGD 350 per night in Singapore and Hong Kong; SGD 220 elsewhere.
- **Meals with clients** — up to SGD 120 per head; attendees must be named.
- **Taxi and ride-hailing** — permitted for business travel and for work after 21:00.
- **Home broadband and mobile** — fixed monthly allowance only; individual bills are not claimable.

## 7. Non-claimable items
Personal entertainment, traffic fines, spouse or family travel, alcohol beyond
a client meal, gifts to public officials, and any expense already reimbursed by
a client.

## 8. Foreign currency
Convert at the Northstar month-end rate published in the Finance SharePoint
site. Do not use a personal card's conversion rate.

## 9. Exceptions and escalation
Exceptions require written Financial Controller approval before the expense is
incurred. Where this policy is silent, escalate to Finance Operations rather
than assuming approval.
