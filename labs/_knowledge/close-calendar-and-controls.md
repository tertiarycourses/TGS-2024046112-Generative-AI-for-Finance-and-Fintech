## 1. Close timetable
All times are Singapore time. "BD" means business day after month-end.

| Step | Deadline | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts payable cut-off | BD1 12:00 | AP Manager |
| Accounts receivable cut-off | BD1 17:00 | AR Manager |
| Bank reconciliation complete | BD2 17:00 | Treasury Analyst |
| Balance-sheet reconciliations complete | BD3 17:00 | Financial Accountant |
| Variance analysis and commentary | BD4 12:00 | FP&A Manager |
| Controller review and sign-off | BD5 17:00 | Financial Controller |
| Management pack issued | BD6 12:00 | FP&A Manager |

## 2. Materiality
- A variance is **material** if it exceeds **SGD 50,000** or **10%** of budget for the line, whichever is lower.
- Every material variance requires a written explanation naming a driver and an owner.
- Immaterial variances are aggregated into an "other" line.

## 3. Reconciliation tolerances
- **Exact match** — amount and reference both agree.
- **Tolerance match** — amount differs by SGD 50 or less; may be auto-cleared with a note.
- **Reference-only match** — amount agrees but the bank narrative lost the invoice ID; requires reviewer confirmation.
- **Exception** — anything else. Must be aged, owned and explained.

Unreconciled items over **SGD 10,000** or older than **60 days** are escalated
to the Financial Controller.

## 4. Journal entries
- All journals require a preparer and an independent reviewer.
- Journals above SGD 100,000 require Financial Controller approval.
- **AI agents must never prepare, approve or post a journal entry.** Agents may
  assemble supporting evidence and draft a proposed narrative only.

## 5. Evidence retention
Close evidence is retained for **7 years**. Each reconciliation must carry the
preparer, reviewer, date and the source extract used.

## 6. Reopening a closed period
A closed period may be reopened only with CFO approval and a documented reason.
